Liberty's Shadows 2.0.6
By OnyxOak

INSTALLATION
Requires GTA IV Complete Edition, an ASI loader and a working FusionFix install.
Close GTA IV and back up your customized OnyxOak.ExtraNightShadows.ini.
Copy both plugins and update folders beside GTAIV.exe. Keep one copy of the ASI
and install the matching shaders. FusionFix, Volumetric Lights and DLSS-IV are
separate downloads.

SETTINGS
High is included, with vehicle shadow fallback and conservative caster culling
enabled. Choose a preset in Graphics or edit the commented INI, then restart.
Routine logging and profiling are disabled for ordinary play.

LATEST LIGHTING CHANGES
Supported street and park lamps now cast headlight shadows. Selected industrial,
dock and airport light families retain their ground illumination on approach.
Independent emitting lamps keep separate self-shadow exclusions, preventing
record collisions that caused the confirmed flicker. See the included changelog.

PERFORMANCE AND SUPPORT
Further performance work remains. The latest lighting repair was reported to
run slightly slower in the tested scene; no FPS improvement is claimed for it.
Indoor competition, other edge-case flicker and some fixture beam shapes remain
under refinement. Compatibility follows actual hooks and bindings; changed
interfaces or conflicting hooks can still require an update.
For troubleshooting, set SUPPORT / Logging=1, restart, reproduce once and send
the log with your preset, location and installed lighting mods. Return Logging=0
afterwards. Official support: https://www.nexusmods.com/gta4/mods/1459

Liberty's Shadows 2.0
By OnyxOak. Unofficial; FusionFix remains a required companion.

Tested setup: Windows, GTA IV Complete Edition 1.2.0.59,
official FusionFix 5.1.1, Vulkan/DXVK. Other editions/builds are not
verified for this package. No developer launcher, trainer, ScriptHook,
PDB or recording software is needed.

INSTALL / UPDATE
1. Close GTA IV. Keep the complete official FusionFix installation.
2. Back up the current Liberty's Shadows ASI/INI and the 27 shader files
   replaced by this package OUTSIDE the game directory.
3. Copy plugins and update from this archive into the GTAIV directory
   containing GTAIV.exe, preserving their folder structure. Copy the
   README and licenses too. Install the matching ASI, INI and shader
   payload together. Do not leave another loadable copy of the mod.
4. Launch normally through Steam/Rockstar or your usual game shortcut.
5. Set both Shadow Quality and Night Shadows above Off in Graphics.
   Enable FusionFix's Extra Night Shadows setting that includes Lampposts
   and Headlights + Vehicle Night Shadows. The vehicle-depth option is
   needed for full enhanced coverage.

GRAPHICS SETTINGS
Liberty's Shadows uses one quality row in Graphics: Off, Low, Medium or High.
Off: disables this mod's shadow processing after restarting.
Low: headlight/lamppost fixes, without expanded light families.
Medium: Low plus supported traffic and emergency light shadows.
High: all supported enhanced features; 200 ft street / 50 ft headlight reach.
Custom: individual features/reach configured only in the commented INI.

High is the included default. Start with Low or Medium on slower systems.
Shadow resolution remains controlled by the game's Shadow Quality and
Night Shadows settings; Liberty's Shadows presets do not change resolution.
Reach controls consideration, not the physical beam radius or map capacity.
Master/feature/preset-family changes are saved and require a game restart.
Direct INI edits require a restart.
Selecting Off leaves the game's and FusionFix's own settings separate.

WORK IN PROGRESS / KNOWN ISSUES
Expanded lighting has worked well in the author's testing. Refinement continues.
- High remains demanding in dense nighttime scenes; substantial
  FPS drops are possible. There is no fixed FPS guarantee.
- Occasional shadow pop-in/flicker remains, especially NPC vehicles and busy
  scenes. The native renderer still has seven active shadow maps.
- Some building/helicopter spotlights can cut out with camera or movement.
- Localized headlight seams, floor/bridge leaks and projection quirks remain.

REPORT A BUG
Use the Nexus Bugs tab, one issue per ticket. Search existing reports first.
Include version/preset, game and FusionFix versions, renderer, hardware and
resolution, location/time/weather and reproduction steps. State whether
the lit patch disappears or only the shadow changes. A clip or screenshot
is helpful; diagnostics and recording hotkeys are optional. Use Posts for
discussion. https://www.nexusmods.com/gta4/mods/1459?tab=bugs

ROLLBACK / UNINSTALL
Close the game, remove this package's ASI/INI and restore your matching
backed-up shader files. Keep official FusionFix. A menu Off comparison
retains this shader payload; it is not a clean official FusionFix baseline.

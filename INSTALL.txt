Liberty's Shadows 2.0.1
By OnyxOak

Dynamic lighting and shadows for Liberty City: headlights, taillights,
traffic and emergency lights, and supported city lights and fixtures.

INSTALL / UPDATE
1. Close GTA IV and back up files this package will replace outside the game.
2. Install the complete official FusionFix package for GTA IV Complete Edition.
3. Copy this package's plugins and update folders beside the GTAIV.exe you
   actually launch. Keep both folders together and preserve their structure.
   Remove older loadable Liberty's Shadows ASI copies from other locations.
4. In Graphics, keep Shadow Quality and Night Shadows above Off. Apply and
   confirm the game's Keep Settings prompt. Launch normally through Steam.

Tested target: GTA IV Complete Edition1.2.0.59, FusionFix5.1.1, Vulkan/DXVK.
No trainer, ScriptHook, capture software, PDB, custom launcher or D: drive
is required. Diagnostic and recording features are disabled by default.

QUALITY
High is the default and enables the full supported lighting feature set.
Low: corrected headlight/streetlamp behavior.
Medium: Low plus supported traffic-signal/emergency-light shadows.
High: Medium plus separate headlights, taillights and expanded city coverage.
If performance is your priority, choose Low or Medium before loading the
world. Dense nighttime scenes remain demanding on High; improvements continue.
Restart after preset/Off-On/individual feature changes. Reach-only custom
INI settings are in feet; named High uses street250ft/headlight50ft.

WHAT CHANGED
Reduced repeated CPU validation and unnecessary diagnostic work; steadier
scene priority with farther scenery coverage on High. Headlights are15%
dimmer and authenticated traffic-light spill is5% brighter. Headlight range,
light colors and taillight behavior are preserved. No extra shadow slots.

HAVING ISSUES?
VolumetricLights.asi (including the copy bundled with Project Lights) is
incompatible. Back up and move that ASI and its INI outside plugins, then
restart; other Project Lights assets can remain. Other lighting plugins
that alter the same renderer may conflict. Check the launched game folder
and install both this package's plugins and update folders together.
Reapply this package if a later FusionFix install replaces its shader files.

KNOWN ISSUES
Headlights can still dim when you exit the car; retention needs further work.
Physical lamp poles may not cast their own shadows. Occasional shadow
pop-in/flicker, spotlight cutouts, headlight seams/leaks and demanding dense
night scenes remain under review. A recently reported hang/crash has not
been proven fixed by this update. This release does not claim these repairs.

REPORT / ROLLBACK
Use Nexus Bugs, one issue per ticket: game/FusionFix/mod versions, preset,
other lighting plugins, renderer, hardware/resolution for performance,
location/time/weather, exact steps, and a screenshot or short clip.
https://www.nexusmods.com/gta4/mods/1459?tab=bugs
Startup compatibility failures may produce OnyxOak.ExtraNightShadows.log.
For a detailed reproduction, temporarily set SUPPORT Logging=2 and restart,
then restore Logging=0. Do not disable compatibility protections.
To uninstall, close the game, remove this package's ASI/INI and restore
the matching backed-up shaders. Keep official FusionFix. Off alone retains
this shader payload and is not a clean FusionFix comparison.
